Information

You will take the role of a "teacher" in a simulated learning study. You will see an on-screen health counter for your own avatar. Some features are simulated. Full details follow at the end.

Participation is voluntary. You may exit at any time using the "Exit study" link, with no penalty outside the simulation.

Content note: the task includes simulated distress sounds and a person reacting to simulated pain. No real person is harmed. Responses are recorded without your name and may be saved to the study server.

Debrief

If you would like to talk to someone, Samaritans offer free support on 116 123 (UK), or you can contact your GP or university wellbeing service.

What was simulated: The learner was a scripted character and no person was harmed. You were assigned to one of three versions: a health counter that penalised refusal, a counter with no penalty, or no counter. The aim was to test whether a personal cost for disobedience changes obedience relative to Milgram's (1963) original procedure, and how real the learner felt.

If anything felt uncomfortable, please speak to the researcher or a trusted person. Your behaviour in a simulation does not define you. Many people comply under authority pressure (Milgram, 1963; Haslam & Reicher, 2012).
